VoluStep is an offline mapper for your phone's physical volume buttons. Draw a visual curve to decide how each press moves through Android's media-volume indices.

Key features
• Set the number of presses from mute to maximum independently from the number of curve control points
• Drag and snap control points to design a discrete mapping on integer coordinates
• Use short-press steps and an adjustable repeat interval for a held button
• See the current system media volume on the curve
• Add fixed-volume buttons that work without turning on physical-button mapping
• Keep every setting on-device, with no account, ads, or network access

Why VoluStep uses an Accessibility service
VoluStep uses an Accessibility service to detect volume-up and volume-down KeyEvents from the phone's physical buttons. Android may also deliver other physical-key events to the service; VoluStep checks the key code and immediately ignores and passes through non-volume keys. When Android and the phone manufacturer actually deliver volume-button events, the app can apply your mapping in other apps, on the Home screen, or on the lock screen. Some manufacturers may limit button delivery in the background or on the lock screen.

The service does not read window content, on-screen text, touch input, screenshots, passwords, account information, or content from other apps. It participates in button handling only after you accept the in-app prominent disclosure, explicitly grant access in system settings, and turn on VoluStep. Turn off VoluStep or its Accessibility service to restore the system's default button behavior.

Compatibility boundaries
VoluStep controls Android's public integer media-volume index, not real decibels, an earphone's internal gain, or actual sound-pressure level. The available steps and perceived loudness depend on the phone, ROM, output route, and audio device.

Volume buttons or touch controls on an earphone are commonly handled directly by the Bluetooth stack or earphone firmware and may bypass VoluStep. Bluetooth absolute volume, LE Audio, fixed-volume devices, manufacturer battery policies, and system button combinations can also affect results. The app does not bypass safe-volume warnings, Do Not Disturb, or manufacturer restrictions.

Test VoluStep with your own phone and earphones before deciding whether to keep it enabled.
